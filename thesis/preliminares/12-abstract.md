# ABSTRACT

**DAVID ENRIQUE OCHOA DIAZ**

The KaabLab project aims to adapt, retrain, and validate under real conditions an intelligent hive monitoring system for *Melipona beecheii* based on the Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI). The reference pipeline (Chin-Canché et al., 2026) was validated on *Apis mellifera* data from California and has not been adapted to this species or to the tropical climate of Campeche; its computer-vision module for *Varroa destructor* detection is not transferable, since Varroa does not parasitize *Melipona beecheii*, and is excluded.

The system integrates a bioacoustic analysis module —based on Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC), structural vibration, and environmental variables— and an infrared traffic-counting module at the hive entrance as a proxy for foraging. As an original design contribution, a heterogeneous sensor architecture with complementary ranges is incorporated: two temperature and humidity sensors (internal primary and external secondary), a microphone for acoustic level and overall colony status, an accelerometer for nest vibration, and a dual-beam infrared sensor for directional counting. This architecture eliminates common failure modes, covers the high relative humidity range (>85% RH) where the external sensor degrades, enables the internal-external diagnostic contrast characteristic of a thermoconforming species, and captures the structural vibrations through which other stingless bees express defensive behavior (undocumented in *Melipona beecheii*), which are not directly recordable with a microphone (substrate vibration).

This thesis closes three gaps in previous work: the lack of data specific to *Melipona beecheii*, the lack of regionalization to the tropical climate, and the absence of documented signal preprocessing prior to feature extraction. It also builds the first documented multimodal dataset for the species and characterizes five bibliographic gaps in its measurement parameters (section 1.1.5).

**Keywords: **Mayan stingless beekeeping, *Melipona beecheii*, intelligent hive monitoring, heterogeneous sensor architecture, sensor redundancy, signal preprocessing, weighted sensor fusion, bioacoustics, structural vibration, infrared traffic counting, IoT, XGBoost.
